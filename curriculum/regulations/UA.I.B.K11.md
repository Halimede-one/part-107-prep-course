Objective: know what is required when a visual observer is used, and what a visual observer does not change.

A visual observer is optional. The remote pilot in command may use one or more to supplement situational awareness and visual line of sight. When one is used, 107.33 sets three requirements. The remote pilot in command, the person manipulating the flight controls, and the visual observer must maintain effective communication with each other at all times. The remote pilot in command must ensure that the visual observer is able to see the aircraft in the manner 107.31 describes. And the three must coordinate to scan the airspace where the aircraft is operating for any potential collision hazard, and to maintain awareness of the aircraft's position through direct visual observation.

AC 107-2A fills in the practice. The remote pilot in command positions the observer where the observer can see the aircraft. The method of communication is decided before the operation and must not create a distraction; a handheld radio is acceptable. The observer reports the aircraft's location, attitude, altitude, and direction of flight, the position of other aircraft or hazards, and whether the aircraft endangers the life or property of another. A visual observer needs no remote pilot certificate, but is bound by the medical condition rule, the alcohol and drug rules, and the one-aircraft-at-a-time rule.

Worked example: a roof inspection has the pilot at the back of a building and the observer at the front with a radio. Before launch they agree on their calls, such as "aircraft at the chimney, 60 feet, nothing in the air." That meets all three requirements. If the observer walks to the side street and loses sight of the aircraft, the remote pilot in command no longer has an observer who can see it.

Common misreading: thinking the observer relieves the remote pilot in command of the duty to be able to see the aircraft. Under 107.31 the remote pilot in command and the person on the controls must keep that capability; the observer supplements it.
