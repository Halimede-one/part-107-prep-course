# Regulations (Area of Operation I): objectives

The canonical list of every knowledge code the Regulations area must cover, transcribed verbatim from FAA-S-ACS-10B (effective April 6, 2021, current in 2026). Every coded line, including the lettered sub-elements, is a tracked unit of the course: mastery is tracked per unit, quiz misses point at units, remediation targets units, and the integrity check uses this list to confirm every unit has curriculum content and a reference-fact entry. Tracking at this full granularity is deliberate; it is the competency signal that tells the tutor exactly which point to re-teach.

Exam weight: Regulations is 15 to 25 percent of the 60-question exam, roughly 9 to 15 questions.

## How to read a code

Example, `UA.I.B.K10`:

- `UA` = ACS for Unmanned Aircraft Systems
- `I` = Area of Operation (Regulations)
- `B` = Task (Operating Rules)
- `K10` = Knowledge element (Visual line of sight (VLOS) aircraft operations)

Lettered sub-elements (for example `K21a`) are tracked as their own units, so a miss localizes to the specific point rather than to the parent. A few parent codes (such as K21, Operating limitations) are pure groupings for their sub-elements; their content frames and links the sub-elements, and their coverage is satisfied by their children.

The six tasks in Area I are: A General, B Operating Rules, C Remote Pilot Certification with an sUAS Rating, D Waivers, E Operations Over People, and F Remote Identification (RID). There are 65 top-level codes plus 12 lettered sub-elements, for 77 tracked units in total across the six tasks.

## How to read a unit number

People use a short number in place of the code: chapter.lesson. The course map and the tutor show both, for example `2.16 (UA.I.B.K16)`.

- The chapter is the task. Task A is chapter 1, B is 2, C is 3, D is 4, E is 5, and F is 6.
- The lesson is the knowledge element number, with its sub-element letter if it has one.

Examples: `UA.I.A.K5` is 1.5, `UA.I.B.K16` is 2.16, `UA.I.B.K21a` is 2.21a, and `UA.I.E.K3c` is 5.3c. The later ACS areas continue the chapter count: Area II starts at chapter 7, Area III at 9, Area IV at 11, and Area V at 12. The number is only a label. The code stays the name of the lesson files and the key in the tables.
