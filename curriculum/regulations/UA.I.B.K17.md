Objective: know the rule against interfering with airport operations, and how it differs from the airspace authorization rule.

Section 107.43 says that no person may operate a small unmanned aircraft in a manner that interferes with operations and traffic patterns at any airport, heliport, or seaplane base. The rule does not depend on airspace class. Around a towered airport the pilot also needs prior authorization under 107.41 for the controlled airspace, but at an airport in uncontrolled airspace, where no authorization or notification is needed, 107.43 still applies in full.

AC 107-2A explains what interference looks like. The remote pilot in command must be aware of and avoid traffic patterns and the approach corridors to runways and landing areas, and must avoid any area where the aircraft could interfere with operations, such as approach corridors, taxiways, runways, or helipads. The small unmanned aircraft must yield the right of way to all other aircraft, including aircraft operating on the surface of the airport. Interference does not require a collision hazard: the AC's example is a small unmanned aircraft hovering 200 feet above a runway, which may cause a manned aircraft holding short to delay its takeoff or an aircraft on the downwind leg to delay its landing. That is interference with the traffic pattern, even though no right-of-way rule was broken. When operational necessity requires flying at an uncontrolled airport, the pilot should fly so that no manned aircraft pilot has to alter course in the pattern or on an instrument approach.

Worked example: a pilot photographs a hangar at an untowered airport in Class G airspace. No authorization is needed, but the pilot keeps the aircraft outside the traffic pattern and away from the approach paths, and lands when an aircraft enters the pattern.

Common misreading: thinking that an airspace authorization settles the question. Authorization under 107.41 permits flight in the controlled airspace; it does not permit interference with the airport's operations under 107.43.
