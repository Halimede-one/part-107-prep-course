Objective: know the rule on transponders in Part 107.

Section 107.52 says that unless otherwise authorized by the Administrator, no person may operate a small unmanned aircraft system under Part 107 with a transponder on. A transponder is the equipment manned aircraft use to reply to air traffic control radar. The rule does not forbid carrying the equipment; it forbids operating with it on. The only way around the rule is an authorization from the Administrator. The section was added to Part 107 in January 2021.

Remote identification, covered in chapter 6, is the broadcast that small unmanned aircraft use to identify themselves. A transponder is not part of it and does not substitute for it.

Worked example: a pilot fits a surplus aviation transponder to a drone, reasoning that it will make the aircraft more visible to air traffic control. Switching it on during a Part 107 flight breaks 107.52 unless the pilot holds an authorization.

Common misreading: assuming that more equipment is always permitted. Here the equipment is prohibited in operation unless the Administrator authorizes it.
