Objective: know what a means of compliance is, who may propose one, and how it differs from a declaration of compliance.

A means of compliance is the method by which an applicant shows that a small unmanned aircraft meets the safety requirements of 107.120(a) for Category 2 or 107.130(a) for Category 3: the injury-severity limit, the prohibition on exposed rotating parts that would lacerate skin, and the absence of safety defects. Under 107.155 it must consist of test, analysis, or inspection. A person seeking FAA acceptance submits a detailed description of the procedures, including the conditions, environments, and methods, and an explanation of how applying the means of compliance fulfills the requirements. If the FAA finds the submission demonstrates compliance, it notifies the applicant of acceptance. An accepted means of compliance stays under review, and the FAA may rescind its acceptance, with a notice in the Federal Register.

AC 107-2A describes the landscape. Anyone may submit a means of compliance, an individual or a voluntary consensus standards body such as ASTM International or SAE. The FAA judges whether the tests or analyses follow accepted methods used by the medical industry, consumer safety groups, or other peer-reviewed methods, and whether the proposal relies on exceptional pilot skill or excessive workload. The FAA also provides one already-accepted means of compliance, based on calculating the aircraft's maximum impact kinetic energy and confirming that it has no exposed rotating parts and no safety defects. The distinction to hold onto: the means of compliance is the method; the declaration of compliance is the applicant's statement that a particular aircraft meets the requirements using an accepted method. A declaration must name the accepted means of compliance it relied on.

Worked example: a manufacturer measures its aircraft's top speed and free-fall impact speed, calculates the impact kinetic energy with the FAA-provided formula, confirms the propellers are shrouded, and verifies there are no safety defects. That is the application of a means of compliance. The manufacturer then submits a declaration of compliance for the model, naming the FAA-provided means of compliance.

Common misreading: confusing the means of compliance with the declaration. One is the method of showing; the other is the declaration that the method was applied and the aircraft passed.
