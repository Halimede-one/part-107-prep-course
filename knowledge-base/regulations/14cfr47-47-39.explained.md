This section answers a narrow question: when does an aircraft's registration actually take effect? Two points. First, the aircraft is registered on the date the Registry (the FAA Aircraft Registration Branch) determines that the submissions meet the requirements of this part — not when you fill out the paperwork, not when you mail it, but when the Registry decides your filing is complete and correct. Second, you can find that date printed on the Certificate of Aircraft Registration itself, as the date of issue. So the certificate tells you its own effective date.

Illustration: You mail a registration application for an aircraft on March 1. The Registry reviews it, finds the submissions meet the requirements of this part, and issues a Certificate of Aircraft Registration showing a date of issue of March 20. Your registration is effective March 20, not March 1. If you want to know the effective date later, you read the date of issue off the certificate.

Common misreading: People assume registration is effective the day they submitted the application or the day the fee cleared. The chunk says the date is set by the Registry's determination that the submissions meet the requirements, and that date appears as the date of issue on the certificate.
