This section tells the remote pilot in command (the person directly responsible for a given small unmanned aircraft flight) when a flight has to be reported to the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA). Two triggers exist. The first is serious injury to any person, or any loss of consciousness. The second is damage to any property other than the small unmanned aircraft itself. The property trigger has two escape conditions: no report is needed if the cost of repair, including both materials and labor, does not exceed $500, or if the property was a total loss and its fair market value does not exceed $500. If either trigger applies and no escape condition is met, the report must reach the FAA no later than 10 calendar days after the operation, in a manner acceptable to the Administrator. Note that damage to your own small unmanned aircraft is expressly excluded from the property trigger.

Illustration: You are flying a roof inspection and lose control on descent. The aircraft clips a parked car's windshield, then hits the driveway and is destroyed. Nobody is hurt. The body shop quotes $840 in parts and labor to replace the windshield. Your own aircraft is a write-off. Decision: you must file a report with the FAA within 10 calendar days. The windshield is property other than the small unmanned aircraft, and the $840 repair cost exceeds $500, so neither escape condition applies. Your destroyed aircraft does not factor in at all, because the rule excludes the small unmanned aircraft from the property trigger. If the quote had come back at $400, no report would be required under this section.

Common misreading: Two traps. First, people assume a $500 floor applies to injuries too. It does not. The $500 thresholds appear only under paragraph (b), for property. A serious injury or any loss of consciousness is reportable regardless of cost. Second, people count the price of their own crashed drone toward the $500 figure. The chunk excludes the small unmanned aircraft from the property trigger, so a destroyed $3,000 aircraft with no other damage and no injury does not require a report under this section.
