This is a one-line pointer, not a rule. It tells you what study material the knowledge test draws on: Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (14 CFR) part 107, every other part that part 107 refers to, and Advisory Circular (AC) 107-2. The rest of the chunk is a blank page and page numbers, so there is no requirement text here to explain. Note that the chunk does not list which other parts part 107 references or what AC 107-2 contains — see those documents.

Illustration: You are building a study plan for the Remote Pilot certificate test. Following this line, you put part 107 at the center, then follow each cross-reference out of part 107 to the other parts it names and read those too, and you add AC 107-2 as the FAA's own guidance on part 107. You do not stop at part 107 alone, because the chunk says the referenced parts count as well.

Common misreading: People read "be familiar with part 107" and treat part 107 as the whole syllabus. The line explicitly adds the parts that part 107 references and AC 107-2.
