This chunk is mostly back matter: a one-line pointer, a reference list, an appendix of part 48 sections, and the start of an acronym list. The table of acronyms is cut off mid-list, so treat it as a fragment. The teachable points are small. First, Chapter 12 of the study guide does not itself contain the maintenance and preflight inspection material — it tells you that this material is in chapter 7 of Advisory Circular (AC) 107-2, Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems. So if you are studying maintenance or preflight inspection procedures, the study guide sends you to that AC rather than explaining it here. Second, Appendix 1 lists the documents the study guide draws on, including 14 CFR (Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations) parts 47, 48, 71, and 107, several ACs, the Aeronautical Information Manual (AIM), FAA handbooks, the Airman Certification Standards, and four Safety Alerts for Operators (SAFOs). The note tells users to reference the current edition of each listed document, because these publications are revised over time. Third, Appendix 2 says applicants must be familiar with all of the information in 14 CFR part 48, Registration and Marking Requirements for Small Unmanned Aircraft, and especially these sections: 48.15 Requirement to register, 48.20 Eligibility for registration, 48.25 Applicants, 48.100 Application, and 48.205 Display and location of unique identifier. The chunk only names those sections; see those sections for what they actually require.

Illustration: You are preparing for the remote pilot knowledge test and your notes have nothing on how to inspect the aircraft before a flight. You check Chapter 12 and find only a cross-reference. The right move is to open AC 107-2 and read chapter 7, and to download the current edition rather than an older copy you already have, because the study guide's note says to use the current edition of every listed reference. While you are at it, you pull up part 48 and read 48.15, 48.20, 48.25, 48.100, and 48.205, since Appendix 2 flags those as the registration and marking sections to know.

Common misreading: Do not read Appendix 2 as saying the five listed sections are the only part 48 material you need. The chunk says applicants must be familiar with all of the information in part 48, and that those five sections deserve special attention. Also, Appendix 1 is a list of references, not a list of rules — nothing in that table imposes a requirement by itself.
