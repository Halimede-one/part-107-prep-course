This section covers two paperwork tasks for holders of a remote pilot certificate with small UAS (unmanned aircraft system) rating.

Name change: if you apply to change the name on your certificate, your application must come with two things — your remote pilot certificate with small UAS rating, and a copy of the marriage license, court order, or other document that verifies the name change. Those documents get returned to you after the FAA (Federal Aviation Administration) inspects them.

Address change: if your permanent mailing address changes, you have 30 days from the date of that change to notify the FAA. After those 30 days, you may not exercise the privileges of your certificate until you have notified them. Two methods are listed: a letter to the FAA Airman Certification Branch, P.O. Box 25082, Oklahoma City, OK 73125, or the FAA website portal at www.faa.gov. Either way you provide the new permanent mailing address — and if that address includes a post office box number, you provide your current residential address as well.

Illustration: A remote pilot moves across town on March 1 and plans a paid roof inspection flight on April 10. She has not told the FAA about the new address. Because more than 30 days have passed since the move, she may not exercise the privileges of her certificate, so the flight cannot legally go ahead as a Part 107 operation. She goes to the FAA website portal, enters her new permanent mailing address, and since she uses a P.O. box for mail, she also supplies her current residential address. With the notification made, she can exercise her certificate privileges again.

Common misreading: People read the 30 days as a deadline that carries a fine or a late fee. It is not that. The consequence written here is that after 30 days you may not exercise the privileges of the certificate until you notify the FAA — the certificate is effectively unusable for Part 107 flying. Another slip: using a P.O. box as your mailing address does not let you stop there; the chunk requires your current residential address in that case.
