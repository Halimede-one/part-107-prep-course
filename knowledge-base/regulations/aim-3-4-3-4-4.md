A warning area is airspace of defined dimensions, extending from three nautical miles outward from the coast of the U.S., that contains activity that may be hazardous to nonparticipating aircraft. The purpose of such warning
areas is to warn nonparticipating pilots of the potential danger. A warning area may be located over domestic or international waters or both.
