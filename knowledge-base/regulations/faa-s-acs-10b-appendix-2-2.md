Before starting the actual test, the testing center provides an opportunity to practice navigating through the test. This
practice or tutorial session may include sample questions to familiarize the applicant with the look and feel of the software
(e.g., selecting an answer, marking a question for later review, monitoring time remaining for the test, and other features of
the testing software).
