This is a pointer to a resource, not a rule. The chunk tells remote pilots that the website https://www.1800wxbrief.com/ offers additional tools for three preflight tasks: creating NOTAMs (Notices to Airmen, the FAA's system of advisory notices about conditions affecting flight), checking weather, and preparing flight plans. Nothing here requires you to use that site or to file anything — it simply names a place where those services are available.

Illustration: You are planning a mapping flight near a small airport tomorrow morning and want to see whether anything unusual is happening in that airspace and what the weather will look like. Rather than hunting across several sites, you go to https://www.1800wxbrief.com/, where the chunk says NOTAM creation, weather, and flight plan tools are all available. You use it as one stop for that preflight information.

Common misreading: People sometimes read a listed website as a mandatory step — as if NOTAMs must be created or flight plans filed through this particular site. This chunk only identifies where those resources can be found; it states no requirement at all.
