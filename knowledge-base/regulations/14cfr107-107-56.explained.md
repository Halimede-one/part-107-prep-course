This is the opening line of a subpart, and all it does is tell you what the following sections cover: the requirements for issuing a remote pilot certificate with a small UAS (unmanned aircraft system) rating. It contains no limit, test, or duty of its own. Think of it as a signpost. When you need to know how a person gets that certificate, this subpart is where to look; the actual eligibility, application, and testing requirements live in the sections that follow, so see those sections for the substance. The bracketed line at the end is publication history, not a rule.

Illustration: You are helping a coworker who wants to fly a drone for your company and needs a remote pilot certificate. You open Part 107 and land on § 107.56. It tells you only that this subpart is the place where certificate-issuing requirements are written, so you keep reading into the following sections to find the eligibility and knowledge test requirements rather than treating § 107.56 as an answer in itself.

Common misreading: Some readers treat an applicability section as if it granted or limited something. It does not. § 107.56 neither qualifies nor disqualifies anyone; it only describes the scope of the subpart that follows.
