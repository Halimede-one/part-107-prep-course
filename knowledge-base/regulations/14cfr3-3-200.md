section: § 3.200 Effect of Transportation Security Administration notification on a certificate or any part of a certificate held by an individual
url: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/section-3.200
page: 2
kind: source
---
When the Transportation Security Administration (TSA) notifies the FAA that an individual holding a certificate or part of a certificate issued by the FAA poses, or is suspected of posing, a risk of air piracy or terrorism or a threat to
airline or passenger safety, the FAA will issue an order amending, modifying, suspending, or revoking any certificate or part of a certificate issued by the FAA.
