This is a fragment — just the applicability line opening a chapter of Advisory Circular (AC) 107-2A. It tells you what the chapter that follows will cover: eligibility requirements for small unmanned aircraft and the responsibilities of the remote pilot when flying over people. No actual requirement appears in this chunk; the substance is in the paragraphs that follow this heading, so see that chapter for the rules themselves.

Illustration: A remote pilot who wants to fly a small drone above a crowd at a downtown plaza opens AC 107-2A looking for direction. Reaching this applicability line, the pilot learns only that this is the right chapter — it addresses both whether the aircraft itself is eligible for operations over people and what the pilot must do. The pilot keeps reading into the chapter to find the specific criteria, because this line alone does not authorize or prohibit the flight.

Common misreading: Do not read "guidance" here as meaning the chapter is optional background. An advisory circular explains how to comply with the rules; the underlying eligibility requirements it describes come from the regulation itself.
