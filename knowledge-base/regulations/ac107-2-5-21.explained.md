The only substance here: Appendix B of this advisory circular holds expanded information on operational topics that the FAA says should be considered before you fly. It is supplemental reading, not a rule, and the chunk does not say what those topics are — see Appendix B itself. The heading that follows tells you the next chapter covers Part 107 Subpart C, remote pilot certification.

Illustration: You are planning your first paid job and want to know what else the FAA thinks you should think through beforehand. Paragraph 5.21 is the pointer: it sends you to Appendix B for the expanded operational material rather than listing it in the chapter body.

Common misreading: Because the sentence says this information "should be considered," some readers treat Appendix B as a binding checklist. The chunk presents it as supplemental information in an advisory circular, not as a regulatory requirement.
