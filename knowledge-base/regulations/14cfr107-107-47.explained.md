This section is a cross-reference. It says that anyone acting as remote pilot in command must follow the provisions of §§ 91.137 through 91.145 and § 99.7 of this chapter. In other words, those rules from other parts of the regulations apply to you when you fly under Part 107, even though they sit outside Part 107. The section's title tells you the subject matter: flight restrictions near certain areas designated by notice to airmen. The chunk itself does not state what those referenced sections require, so you have to read each of them to know what you must do. See those sections for their content.

Illustration: A remote pilot is hired to photograph storm damage in a neighborhood the day after a hurricane. Before launching, she checks for notices to airmen and finds a temporary flight restriction over the area. Because § 107.47 binds her to §§ 91.137 through 91.145 and § 99.7, she cannot treat those restrictions as something that only applies to crewed aircraft. She looks up the specific referenced section that covers the restriction in question and complies with it, or she does not fly, rather than assuming Part 107 alone governs her flight.

Common misreading: People read Part 107 as a self-contained rulebook and assume that if a requirement is not written inside Part 107, it does not apply to drones. This section says the opposite: it pulls the listed sections from parts 91 and 99 into your obligations as remote pilot in command.
