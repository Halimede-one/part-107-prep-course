This is the cover page, preface, and start of the table of contents for the FAA's Remote Pilot – Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (sUAS) Study Guide, document number FAA-G-8082-22, dated August 2016 and issued by Flight Standards Service. The preface states the document's purpose: the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) published it to communicate the knowledge areas you need to study to prepare for the Remote Pilot Certificate with an sUAS rating airman knowledge test. It also notes the guide can be downloaded from faa.gov and that comments go to afs630comments@faa.gov. No rule or requirement appears in this chunk — it is front matter only.
