This section is the price list for aircraft registration paperwork handled under Part 47. It sets a fee for each type of application: $5.00 for a Certificate of Aircraft Registration (charged per aircraft), $10.00 for a Dealer's Aircraft Registration Certificate, $2.00 for an additional Dealer's certificate issued to the same dealer, $10.00 for a special registration number (per number), $10.00 to change, reassign, or reserve a registration number, $2.00 to replace a Certificate of Aircraft Registration, and $5.00 to renew a Certificate of Aircraft Registration. Paragraph (b) adds the procedural point: the correct fee has to be submitted along with the application, and it may be paid by check or money order made out to the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA). The chunk only lists fees and how to pay them; it does not say who must register or when, which is covered in other sections of this part.

Illustration: You register a drone under Part 47 because you plan to operate it commercially and want a standard N-number certificate. You send in the application with a $5.00 check to the FAA. Two years later the certificate comes up for renewal, so you submit the renewal application with another $5.00. A month after that, your paper certificate is destroyed when water gets into your equipment case. You apply for a replacement, and because this section prices a Replacement Certificate of Aircraft Registration at $2.00, not $5.00, you send $2.00 rather than the original registration amount. Each application goes in with its own matching fee, as paragraph (b) requires.

Common misreading: People often read the $5.00 Certificate of Aircraft Registration fee as covering a whole fleet or a whole company. The line says "each aircraft," so the fee is per aircraft registered. Similarly, the $10.00 special registration number fee is "each number," not one flat charge no matter how many numbers you request. Another trip-up: this fee schedule is for Part 47 registration applications and says nothing about the cost of any other FAA certificate or test.
