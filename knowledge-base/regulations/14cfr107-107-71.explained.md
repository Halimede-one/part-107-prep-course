If you take the aeronautical knowledge test and fail it, you have to wait before you can try again. The rule sets that wait at 14 calendar days after the failure. During those 14 days you cannot reapply for the test. The wait is the only condition this section places on retesting — it applies to any applicant for a knowledge test who fails that test.

Illustration: You sit the initial knowledge test at a testing center on a Monday and come up short of a passing score. You feel ready to try again by Thursday and call the center to book another slot. The center cannot schedule you yet: under this section you may not reapply until 14 calendar days have passed from the day you failed. You use the two weeks to study the areas your test report flagged, then rebook.

Common misreading: People read "14 calendar days" as 14 business days, or assume the wait can be shortened by studying hard, paying a retest fee, or getting someone to vouch for them. Calendar days means every day counts, weekends and holidays included, and this chunk gives no way to waive or shorten the period.
