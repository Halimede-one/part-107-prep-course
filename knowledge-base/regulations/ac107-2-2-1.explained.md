This is a reference list, not a rule. The advisory circular is pointing to the other bodies of regulation that touch small unmanned aircraft operations, and noting they can be found at https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/faa_regulations/. Under Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) — the aviation title — it lists Part 1 (Definitions and Abbreviations), Part 43 (Maintenance, Preventive Maintenance, Rebuilding, and Alteration), Part 47 (Aircraft Registration), Part 48 (Registration and Marking Requirements for Small Unmanned Aircraft), Part 71 (Designation of Class A, B, C, D, and E Airspace Areas; Air Traffic Service Routes; and Reporting Points), Part 73 (Special Use Airspace), Part 89 (Remote Identification of Unmanned Aircraft Systems), Part 91 (General Operating and Flight Rules), Part 93 (Special Air Traffic Rules), Part 99 (Security Control of Air Traffic), and Part 107 (Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems). Outside Title 14 it lists Title 47 CFR Part 87 (Aviation Services), which covers radio, and Title 49 CFR Part 830, which covers notification and reporting of aircraft accidents or incidents and overdue aircraft, and preservation of aircraft wreckage, mail, cargo, and records. The takeaway is that Part 107 is not self-contained: registration, airspace designations, remote identification, and accident reporting live in other parts. This chunk does not state what any of those parts require — see each section for its content.

Illustration: A remote pilot wants to confirm how to mark a registration number on a drone and then wants to know whether a particular area is special use airspace. Neither answer is in Part 107. From this list, the pilot knows to look at Part 48 for registration and marking of small unmanned aircraft, and at Part 73 for special use airspace, plus Part 71 for the Class A through E airspace designations themselves.

Common misreading: Seeing Part 43 (maintenance) and Part 47 (aircraft registration) on this list does not mean they apply to your small unmanned aircraft the way they apply to manned aircraft. The chunk only says these parts are related and where to find them; it does not say which ones govern your operation or how.
