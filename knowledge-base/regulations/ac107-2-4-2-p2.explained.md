This chunk is a reference list, not a rule — it is the abbreviations table from Advisory Circular (AC) 107-2A, listing each acronym used in the AC alongside its spelled-out meaning. There is nothing here to comply with; it only tells you what the letters stand for when you meet them later in the document. A few that come up constantly in small Unmanned Aircraft System (small UAS) work: AGL (Above Ground Level), MSL (Mean Sea Level), VLOS (Visual Line of Sight), VO (Visual Observer), PIC (Pilot in Command), C2 (Command and Control), CS (Control Station), NAS (National Airspace System), NOTAM (Notice to Airmen), TFR (Temporary Flight Restriction), METAR (Aviation Routine Weather Report), TAF (Terminal Aerodrome Forecast), COA (Certificate of Waiver or Authorization), DOC (Declaration of Compliance), MOC (Means of Compliance), and FRIA (FAA-Recognized Identification Area). The list also includes certificate and testing terms (ACS, AKTR, IACRA, FTN, KTC, DPE, ACR), identity-document terms (EDL, EID, ETC, MMC, TWIC), and unit abbreviations (mph for Miles per Hour, sm for Statute Mile).
