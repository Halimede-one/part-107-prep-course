# Regulations: practice exam

The fixed practice exam for the Regulations area. Twenty items, weighted by task roughly as the area's tracked units are distributed (A 10 percent, B 47 percent, C 5 percent, D 1 percent, E 27 percent, F 9 percent), with a floor of one item per task. Scored by exact lookup; the readiness bar is 85 percent on two separate attempts.

Status: draft. Every item must be vetted against the cited section before the exam is used for a readiness decision. Stems and options must not contain the pipe character.
